[Samuel Eckmann](https://samueleckmann.github.io) obtained a PhD at the Max Planck Institute for Brain Research (MPIBR) and the Frankfurt Institute for Advanced Studies (FIAS), working with Julijana Gjorgjieva and Jochen Triesch, respectively. He then became Royal Society Newton International Fellow at the Computational and Biological Learning Lab (CBL) at the University of Cambridge, collaborating with Máté Lengyel and Yashar Ahmadian. He joined the Lab in June 2026.
