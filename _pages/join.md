We are always looking for highly motivated and committed theoreticians and computer scientists.

## Postdocs
{:.subsection-title}

We have open positions for [postdocs](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/work-at-ucl/search-ucl-jobs/details?jobId=28428&jobTitle=Research+Fellow+%28Training+Fellow+in+Theoretical+Neuroscience%29). If you are interested, please follow your formal application with a quick email.

## PhD students
{:.subsection-title}

If you are interested in doing your PhD in the Lab, you can choose between the [Theoretical Neuroscience / Machine Learning PhD](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/gatsby/study-and-work/gatsby-unit-phd-programme) programme and the [joint Gatsby/SWC PhD](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/gatsby/study-and-work/gcnu-swc-joint-phd-programme) programme. The former is more theory-facing and the latter more computation-facing, but through both you can work in collaboration with people at the Sainsbury Wellcome Centre.

## Master's thesis projects
